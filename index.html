Integrating Real Textbooks

Teacher & Developer Workflow

You can compile any high-contrast textbook page, diagram, or worksheet to trigger custom 3D models directly in mobile Safari or Chrome:

1

Capture page: Take a flat photo of the page illustration (e.g. Bohr model, cell division, or heart anatomy).

2

Compile .mind target: Use the free MindAR Image Compiler to export a lightweight .mind descriptor.

3

Link in code: Replace the imageTargetSrc: ... attribute on the <a-scene> element with your compiled file.

Automatic Label Hiding: This app is configured with event hooks that listen to targetFound. When the book is scanned, informative text labels vanish automatically so learners can rotate and inspect clean 3D structures without visual clutter.
