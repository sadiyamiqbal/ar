Troubleshooting & Camera Permissions

Why might camera access fail?

Modern browsers enforce strict security policies:

  • Iframe Restrictions: Inside code playgrounds or sandboxes, webcams require the allow="camera" permission attribute.
  • HTTPS Protocol: Webcams only activate under https:// or localhost.
  • System Permissions: Ensure your OS settings (macOS, Windows, iOS, Android) permit your browser to use the camera.

The 100% Reliable Fallback:

If your camera is blocked or unavailable, switch directly to Photo Page Mode (uploading any picture of your textbook from your camera roll) or 3D Studio Mode to examine and interact with the model with full 360° mouse or touch controls.

Label Auto-Hide Feature:

By default, when a page is recognized, all text tags automatically vanish so learners get an uncluttered, distraction-free 3D visual. Click "Labels: Off / On" in the bottom bar at any time to toggle annotations.
